MIST Study Plan

The day-by-day plan built from the chapter priority map. Every chapter in your syllabus is scheduled once, in an order that builds momentum, peaks with the heaviest chapters, and ends with the lowest-priority ones. That way, if anything slips, what gets squeezed matters least.

Three phases

১. গতি তৈরি

1 Oct – 20 Oct · 20 days

High-value chapters of medium difficulty, plus the foundations the rest depend on (ত্রিকোণমিতি, ভেক্টর, গতিবিদ্যা, গ্যাসের সূত্র). Short wins to build the daily rhythm.

২. শীর্ষ পর্যায়

21 Oct – 29 Nov · 40 days

The heaviest and most-asked chapters: অন্তরীকরণ, যোগজীকরণ, জৈব রসায়ন, রাসায়নিক পরিবর্তন, স্থির তড়িৎ, তড়িৎ চৌম্বক আবেশ. Includes catch-up day 1.

৩. সমাপ্তি

30 Nov – 14 Dec · 15 days

Lower-priority and full-syllabus chapters that are rarely asked. If you fall behind, this is where you cut, not earlier. Ends with catch-up day 2.

How every study day runs

  1. First 15 minutes: yesterday's formulas. Rewrite the previous day's key formulas from memory before opening anything new. This is your only spaced revision until 15 Dec, so don't skip it.
  2. Learn the listed topics in order. The topics on each day come from the priority map, most-asked first. Work the textbook examples for each one.
  3. End every chapter with its MIST questions. Open the priority map, expand the chapter and solve every real question listed there without looking at solutions. Those questions repeat.
  4. Class days (ক্লাস): watch the full-syllabus class and make a one-page formula sheet. The practice happens the next day.
  5. Half days: two short chapters or chapter ends share one day. Finish the first before starting the second.
  6. Last 10 minutes: tick the box and tell me in chat. If the day didn't finish, say what's left and we'll adjust.

Endgame

১৫–১৭ ডিসেম্বর

Revision and model tests only. One full timed paper each morning, then fix every mistake from the chapter's formula sheet. Solve the Repeats list from the priority map again.

১৮ ডিসেম্বর

Pure mental rest. No new problems. Sleep on time.

If you fall behind

  1. Never skip a day's chapter to stay on the calendar. Finish it, then use the next catch-up day (16 Nov, 14 Dec) to recover.
  2. More than 2 days behind at 16 Nov: cut in this order, and only this far: যোগাশ্রয়ী প্রোগ্রাম to a half day, বাস্তব সংখ্যা ও অসমতা to a half day, then জ্যোতির্বিজ্ঞান and the fluid part of পদার্থের গাঠনিক ধর্ম to formulas only.
  3. Never cut a Tier 1 chapter, the MIST questions at the end of a chapter, or 15–18 December.
